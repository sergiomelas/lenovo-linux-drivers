--- Issue Body ---
Hey, I noticed that the modern IdeaPad Slim series is completely missing so far. I have also noticed that the path is very different. Through a lot of testing I figured out that the entry to watch would be:

`\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0.F1CS`
(there also is `\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0.F2CS` for a second fan, which is missing in my device, as well as `\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0.F1TS` and `\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0.F2TS` for their respective target speeds)
The register-width is 8-bit and the multiplier is 100.

Further, those values are mapped into an ACPI `SystemMemory` region named `ECMB` (MMIO at `0xFE802000`).

This definition can't be found inside the DSDT but resides inside SSDT22. I will still attach both files here:
[dsdt+ssdt22_83j3.zip](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/32309066/dsdt%2Bssdt22_83j3.zip)

I can also provide a PR and patch to this driver, adding the quirk for the model myself, if you like.
