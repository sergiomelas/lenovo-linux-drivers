### Environment

- Fedora, kernel `7.2.13-200.fc44.x86_64`
- Mainline `yogafan` is present (`/lib/modules/.../kernel/drivers/hwmon/yogafan.ko.xz`) and loaded (`lsmod` shows it)

### What happens

The module loads and matches the generic `Yoga` family quirk, but no `yogafan`
hwmon device appears under `/sys/class/hwmon/`. As far as I can tell from reading
the source, `probe()` finds no fan handle and returns `-ENODEV`.

### Why (from the DSDT)

The `Yoga` quirk looks for `\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0.FANS`. My decompiled DSDT
(`iasl -d`, attached as `dsdt.dat`) has the EC at that path but defines no
`FANS`, `FAN0`, `FA1S` or `FA2S`, and no tach/RPM named object.

What it does have under `\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0`:

- `FANG(Arg0)` / `FANW(Arg0, Arg1)`, thin wrappers around `EIDR` / `EIDW`
  (EC indexed read/write routines, with a `FAMX` mutex)
- EC status bits `FAN1`, `FAN2`, `FANT`, `FANU`, `FSSN` in the EC memory-mapped
  window (`SystemMemory` at `0xFE0B0800`)

Other EC notes that may help with mapping: the EC uses ports 0x62/0x66, `_GPE` 0x03,
and the battery/thermal fields (`CTML`, `BAM0`, `BDC0`, ...) all live in that
`0xFE0B0800` MMIO window rather than the `EmbeddedControl` region.

### Ask

Could a quirk for this model be added, or could you tell me whether `FANG` /
`EIDR` is the intended path to read fan speed on this EC? Happy to test patches
and report `sensors` / `dmesg` output.
